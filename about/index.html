About Kynoss Studios

An independent iPhone app studio in Florida. We make small, focused apps and keep them maintained long after launch.

Kynoss Studios LLC is a Florida company founded in 2026 by Steven Knauss. It makes daily games, money tools and home and family organizers for iPhone: 15 on the App Store today, with 2 more on the way.

The apps share a few rules. They work without an account. They carry no ads and no third-party trackers. Where a feature needs the internet, the privacy policy says exactly what is sent and where. Prices are shown up front, and the free versions are worth using on their own.

The studio is one developer working with Claude, Anthropic's AI, which writes and tests most of the code. Here's how that works. We also build custom AI systems around the way people work, from condo boards to wedding planning.

On the App Store, our apps are listed under the developer name Steven Knauss.
