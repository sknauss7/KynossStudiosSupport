About Depositt

Moving out of a rental? Depositt walks you room by room and turns dated before-and-after photos into one PDF that shows your landlord what changed, and what was already there.

Deposit disputes usually come down to your memory against your landlord's. The fix is simple: document the place when you move in, document it again when you move out, and show the two side by side. Depositt makes that easy.

How it works

  • Guided walkthrough — Depositt takes you room by room under a numbered room legend, so nothing gets missed.
  • Stamped photos — photos you take in Depositt are stamped with the date and time, and the location if you allow it. Photos you import from your library are marked as imported, so anyone reading the report can tell the two apart.
  • Before & after pairing — at move-out, Depositt lays your move-in photo over the camera so you can line up the same shot, then pairs the two side by side with their dates and captions.
  • A report you can send — a dated PDF with a cover page, the room legend, and every photo shown whole with its stamp and caption.

Free and Pro

Your move-in report is free to make and export, with a small Depositt watermark. Depositt Pro is a one-time in-app purchase: it exports the move-out before & after report and removes the watermark from every report. No subscription.
