About Depositt

Moving out of a rental? Depositt walks you room by room and turns dated before-and-after photos into a clean PDF that proves the place's condition — so you get your security deposit back.

More than a quarter of renters lose part of their deposit, and most never get a clear reason why. The fix is simple: document the place when you move in, document it again when you move out, and show the proof side by side. Depositt makes that effortless.

How it works

  • Guided walkthrough — Depositt prompts you room by room so nothing gets missed, under a numbered room legend.
  • Source-stamped photos — every shot is captured in-app and stamped with the date, time, and (optionally) location. No editing the clock after the fact.
  • Before & after pairing — at move-out, Depositt shows your move-in photo as a ghost overlay so you can line up the exact same shot, then pairs them side by side.
  • One clean PDF — export a dated report with a cover page, room legend, and paired comparisons to send your landlord and keep for your records.

Built for renters

Depositt works for you, the renter — not your landlord. Your photos and reports stay private on your device and sync only across your own devices through iCloud. No account to create, no sign-in.

Document once. Prove the condition. Keep your deposit.
