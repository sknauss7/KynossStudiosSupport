The jobs it takes on

A system isn't a chat window. It's connected to where your work arrives, runs on its own schedule and checks with you on the things you want to decide.

Replies to customers
Texts and email drafted in your customer's language, ready to send with a tap, or sent on their own once you trust them. Anything tricky comes to you.
Books and reminds
Appointments confirmed, reminders sent the day before, and a change request answered with real open times to choose from.
Files and keeps track
