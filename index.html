How it works

We start by learning how you already work, so you don't have to learn a new system.

  1. Tell us the ideaA sentence is enough: the thing you keep losing track of, the job that eats your evenings, the question you wish you could just ask.
  2. We map how the work happensWhere information arrives, what decisions you make, who else is involved and what a great result looks like to you.
  3. You decide what it handlesWhat it does on its own, what it checks with you first and how it reaches you. It's your system, set up your way.
  4. We build it and switch it onYou see it working on your real day-to-day, then it grows one piece at a time as your needs do.

Expect about 7 hours of your time over the first month, most of it in the first week. Tell us about the job and we'll say honestly whether a system would help.
