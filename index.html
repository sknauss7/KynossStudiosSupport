We don't want your data

Every app is built to know as little about you as possible: no accounts, no ads, no third-party trackers, and we never sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Your data stays yours
Most features run on your iPhone. Syncing, sharing and multiplayer go through Apple's iCloud and Game Center, never an account with us.
No ads, no trackers
None of the apps contain advertising or third-party analytics code.
Cloud AI only where we say so
Two apps send AI questions to OpenAI through a relay we run, which doesn't keep them. See what each app sends.
Prices you can read
Free versions are useful on their own. Upgrades are one-time purchases or clearly priced subscriptions, listed on every app page.

One developer and Claude

Every app here is built with Claude, working through a written queue of instructions: code, tests, screenshots, uploads. I decide what gets built and what ships.
