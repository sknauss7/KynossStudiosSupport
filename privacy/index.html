Privacy across our apps

Kynoss Studios is built to know as little about you as possible: no accounts, no ads, no third-party trackers, and we never sell your data or share it with advertisers. The table shows where each app's data goes, and each policy spells it out.

No accounts
None of the apps ask you to sign up. Where they sync, share or play with friends, they use Apple's iCloud and Game Center.
No ads or trackers
No advertising code, no third-party analytics and no cross-app tracking in any app.
Cloud AI only where it's listed
Apps that send anything to an AI service say so below and in their policy, including what is sent and how long it's kept.
You know where it lives
Each policy says what is stored where and how to delete it. Deleting an app removes its data from your iPhone; anything synced to iCloud stays under your Apple account until you remove it.
