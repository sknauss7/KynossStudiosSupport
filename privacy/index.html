Privacy across our apps

The same rules apply to every app. Where an app works differently, the table says so, and its privacy policy spells out exactly how.

No accounts
None of the apps ask you to sign up. Where they sync or share, they use your own iCloud or Game Center.
No ads or trackers
No advertising code, no third-party analytics and no cross-app tracking in any app.
Cloud AI only where it's listed
Apps that send anything to an AI service say so below and in their policy, including what is sent and how long it's kept.
Deleting the app deletes the data
We hold no copy of what you keep in the apps. What stays in your iCloud is yours to remove.
