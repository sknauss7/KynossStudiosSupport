Privacy across our apps

Our apps are made by Kynoss Studios LLC ("we") and built to know as little about you as possible: no accounts, no ads, no third-party trackers, and we never sell your data or share it with advertisers. The table shows where each app's data goes, and each policy spells it out.

No accounts
None of the apps ask you to sign up. Where they sync, share or play with friends, they use Apple's iCloud and Game Center.
No ads or trackers
No advertising code, no third-party analytics and no cross-app tracking in any app.
Cloud AI only where it's listed
Apps that send anything to an AI service say so below and in their policy, including what is sent and how long it's kept.
You know where it lives
Each policy says what is stored where and how to delete it. Deleting an app removes its data from your iPhone; anything synced to iCloud stays under your Apple account until you remove it.

The contact form

When you write to us through the form on our home page, we receive your name, the email or phone number you give, your business name if you add it, and your message. We use them only to reply to you and to talk about working together. We don't sell them or share them with anyone for marketing.

The form is handled by a small service we run on Cloudflare, Inc. Your message waits there until we move it into our own private records, usually within a day, and is then deleted from Cloudflare. We don't keep your IP address with your message; it's used only for one hour, in a scrambled form, to stop the form being flooded. Ask us at any time to delete what you sent: support@kynossstudios.com.

Example pages we make for businesses

Sometimes we make a private example page for a business we'd like to work with, at demo.kynossstudios.com. It uses only public information about that business. It isn't linked from this site, it's hidden from search engines, and we take it down after a set time.

When an example page is opened, we count the visit: which page, the day, and whether it was opened on a phone or a computer. That's all. We don't use cookies, and we don't keep IP addresses. The pages run on Cloudflare, Inc. Ask us to take a page down, or to delete its visit count, at any time: support@kynossstudios.com.

Calls and texts to our business line

When you call or text our business number, we receive your phone number, the time, and what you say or write. If you leave a message, we also receive what you tell us about yourself and your business. We use this only to answer you, to get back to you, to check and improve how our business line answers, and, if you're a client, to support the system we built for you.

An AI assistant answers our calls. It tells you so at the start of the call, along with a notice that the call is transcribed. It can answer questions about our services, take a message, or put your call through to our team. We keep a written transcript of each call, including the part after we put you through, but never the audio. Texts may be translated by AI so that we can read and reply in your language; we keep the original alongside the translation.

We review how our line handles each call and text. After each one, an AI model checks it against our own rules for the line. We keep that review for one year, and it holds no phone number or name. When a text comes from a number we don't know, an AI model also sorts it (for example, a person, spam or a wrong number), so that the people who need a reply reach us quickly. We keep every text, whatever the sort.

Three service providers handle calls and texts for us, only to provide this service to us:

  • Twilio Inc., which carries the calls and texts and turns speech into text;
  • Anthropic PBC, whose Claude models answer, summarize, sort, review and translate;
  • Cloudflare, Inc., which runs the small service that connects them.

The messages and transcripts are then kept in our own private records.

We keep calls and texts for one year after our last contact with you, then delete them. If you become a client, your service agreement sets how long we keep them instead.

Texts: message frequency varies with the conversation. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts from us, and HELP for help. Our text messaging terms have the details.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party. We don't sell your information.

Ask us at any time to see or delete your calls and texts: support@kynossstudios.com.
