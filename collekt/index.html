About Collekt

Collekt counts the moments other trackers miss — the temptation you resisted, the thing you did anyway.

Most habit apps ask you to plan a list, then punish you when you fall off it. Collekt does the opposite. It's for the unscheduled disciplinary moments — the late-night snack you skipped, the workout you did despite the resistance, the hard email you finally sent.

Tap once. Optionally jot a one-line note. Watch the marbles fill your jar. No streaks. No miss to punish. The lifetime tally never resets.

How it works

  • Log: tap +1 (Resisted or Completed) and watch your accumulated moments fill a gravity-fill jar with tangible weight
  • History: scroll the last 14 days (or all of it, with Pro)

Free

  • Unlimited logging
  • History of your last 14 days (your jar and lifetime counts always include every moment)
  • Marbles visualization
  • Home Screen widget — your jar count at a glance
  • Optional chain display (off by default — the lifetime tally never resets either way)
  • JSON export/import (your data, on your phone, portable)

COLLEKT PREMIUM ($4.99, one-time, no subscription)

  • Four additional visualization modes — Paperclips, Currency, Rocket, Garden — each designed for the Resisted/Completed asymmetry
  • Marble shelf — fill a jar, shelve it, start another; one jar becomes a collection
  • Your full History — scroll back past 14 days, two weeks at a time

Privacy

Collekt has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. All data stays on your device. Apple's StoreKit handles purchases; we receive nothing else.
