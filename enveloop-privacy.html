Privacy Policy for Enveloop

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

Introduction

Enveloop ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our approach to data collection and usage.

Data Collection

We collect zero data.

Enveloop stores your financial information on your device using Apple's Core Data framework and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud account. We have no servers, no backend, no analytics, no tracking, and absolutely no access to your data.

Information Stored on Your Device

The following information is stored locally on your device:

  • Your paycheck amount and frequency
  • Your account balances
  • Bills you create (name, amount, due date, recurrence, notes, icon)
  • Envelope allocation amounts
  • Transaction and balance history
  • App settings and preferences (such as appearance/dark mode)

This data stays on your device and in your own iCloud account (see the iCloud section below). It never reaches us.

Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not share any data because we do not have access to any data.

Enveloop does not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • Crash reporting services
  • Social media integrations
  • Bank connections or account linking
  • Any third-party SDKs or libraries that collect data

iCloud Sync & Family Sharing

Enveloop uses Apple's CloudKit to sync your data across your devices. If you're signed in to iCloud with iCloud enabled for Enveloop, your budget data is stored in your private iCloud account and synced automatically. To keep data on this device only, turn off iCloud for Enveloop in iOS Settings.

You can also share a budget account with family members. When you do, the shared account data is stored in Apple's iCloud infrastructure and synced between the people you invite. This data is protected by Apple's iCloud security and is not accessible to us. iCloud is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications in Settings, Enveloop asks for permission to send bill-due and payday reminders. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device; no server is involved.

In-App Purchase

Enveloop offers a 2-week free trial followed by a one-time $4.99 in-app purchase (US price; your App Store shows your local price). Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit framework. We do not collect or store any payment information — all billing is managed by Apple through your Apple ID.

Data Security

Your data is protected by iOS's security measures:

  • Your data is protected by your device passcode/biometrics
  • Data is automatically encrypted by iOS when your device is locked
  • Data synced through iCloud is encrypted by Apple in transit and at rest
  • We cannot access, recover, or reset your data

Your Rights and Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • View: All your data is visible within the app
  • Edit: You can modify any information at any time
  • Delete Individual Items: Delete bills or reset allocations as needed
  • Delete All Data: Use "Reset All Data" in Settings to erase everything
  • Uninstall: Deleting the app removes all data from your device; data synced to your iCloud stays in your iCloud account until you delete it there (iOS Settings > your name > iCloud)

Children's Privacy

Enveloop does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because we don't collect any information at all.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how the app handles data, we will:

  • Update this privacy policy
  • Update the "Last Updated" date
  • Notify users through an app update description

We will never retroactively change our core privacy principle: your data stays with you.

California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have certain rights. However, since Enveloop does not collect any personal information, these provisions do not apply.

European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), EU residents have certain rights. However, since Enveloop does not process any personal data, these provisions do not apply.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@kynossstudios.com · Enveloop Support

Summary

Your financial data is private. Period.

Enveloop was built from the ground up with privacy as the foundation. We believe your financial information belongs to you and you alone.
