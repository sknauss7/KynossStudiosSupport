About Pandemoni

Turn any TV into a party game stage. One phone hosts, everyone else joins by scanning a QR code — no downloads, no accounts, no internet required. All gameplay runs locally between devices on your network.

Two games included. Unlock the rest one at a time, or by the pack.

From bluffing to drawing to dungeon crawling — one app covers the whole game night.

Social deduction

  • Ruse — One spy doesn't know the secret location. Find them before they blend in.
  • Guise — One player doesn't know the secret word. Blend in or get caught.
  • Feral — Werewolves hunt at night. Village votes by day. 19 optional roles.
  • Syndicate — Mafia with 31 roles, an untouchable Godfather, and a noir twist.
  • Scheme — Everyone builds on the same stacks for a secret motive. Read theirs and burn it first.
  • Snare — Take words off one wall. Your secret category pays double, until someone locks it.

Creative & icebreaker

  • Bedlam — Wild prompts, wilder answers. Submit and vote on the best.
  • Fable — You tell two truths and a lie. Others argue your story — even the lie.
  • Forgery — Draw together, one line at a time. One artist is faking it.
  • Void — Name things nobody else will. Match someone and you both score nothing.
  • Hoax — Write a lie the room believes. Then pick out the one true answer.
  • Reprise — Clue your secret prompt in five words, then one word, then one emoji.
  • Tatter — Slide story scraps into the gaps. Pick the gap everyone wants and the seam tears.
  • Garble — Answer with thirty word tiles. Every word you play is gone for good.

Strategy & trivia

  • Torrent — Speed trivia. Answer fast for more points.
  • Gambit — Bid your points on risky scenarios. Go big or go broke.
  • Havoc — Name things in a category before the timer explodes.
  • Brink — Climb the ladder for more points. Bank before it snaps under you.
  • Daunt — Take the dare, stake your nerve. Two witnesses rule on whether you did it.
  • Ballpark — Everyone guesses a number. Then bet on who came closest without going over.
  • Decree — Throw a law at a friend. Break the one you're wearing and its author cashes in.
  • ReGift — Give a treasure away every round. Finish someone's three and the Eye smashes them.

Logic & teamwork

  • Mines — Give one-word clues to guide your team across a minefield.
  • Axiom — Six guesses at a hidden rule. Feed the machine a card and watch wrong ones crack.
  • Bastion — Say your clue for a little, or hold it and hope the room saves the road without you.
  • Mosaic — Publish one clue, keep the other. A wrong marker can hand the table a free fact.

Party & opinion

  • Salvo — Rank topics and predict the group consensus.
  • Bossy — You're famous but don't know who. Ask questions, guess your identity.
  • Pact — Negotiate alliances in real time. Betray at your own risk.
  • Schism — Where do you fall between two extremes? Place your marker.
  • Hunch — Bet on how well you know your friends. The surer you are, the more it costs.
  • Hive — The obvious answer wins. Score by writing what everyone else wrote.

Adventure

  • Delve — Four rooms and a boss. Clear every one and still lose your secret quest.
  • Vault — Five rooms, one kit. Every tool that opens a lock is one you don't get to keep.

Game night mode

Play multiple games back-to-back with persistent win tracking. Players join once and stay connected — switch modes between rounds.

Works with any TV

AirPlay 2 smart TVs, USB-C to HDMI adapters, or Apple TV (universal purchase — buy once, play everywhere).

New in 1.5.0

Six new games in Pandemonium Pack 3, which brings Pandemoni to 34.

Garble: build every answer out of your own word tiles, and your rack shrinks with every answer. Scheme: one shared board of stacks, a secret goal for each of you, and every block you drop shows the room what you want. Tatter: slip scraps into a story with its middle missing, and hope nobody else picked the same gap. Snare: grab words off one wall, cash in on a secret category, and fire locks at whoever you think is chasing theirs. Decree: slap a secret rule on another player. If they obey it, you both score. If they break it, you collect. ReGift: hand a treasure to a neighbor every round. Give someone their third of a kind and the whole set gets smashed.

The Shop now gives every pack its own full-width row, so each name and saving reads in full. And if one item can't load, the rest of the Shop still opens.
