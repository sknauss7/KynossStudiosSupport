About Sirrius

Keep your pet's health organized, calm, and yours.

Sirrius keeps vaccinations, medications, weight, vet visits, and symptom journals in one place. Ask in plain English — "can my dog eat almonds?" gets an instant, severity-coded answer from a 200+ item safety database that works offline. It spots patterns in symptoms over time, builds breed-aware care schedules, and tells you what delayed care could cost — so you stay ahead instead of catching up.

Invite your household so everyone sees and updates the same records — meds, vet visits, weight, and symptoms. Only people you invite can join. Export a complete health record PDF for your vet. No account needed.

What you get, free

  • Pet profiles with vaccination and medication tracking for up to 2 pets
  • Medication and care-due reminders
  • Food and plant safety checker with 200+ items — works offline
  • Weight history with charts
  • Household sharing by invite, through iCloud
  • Health record PDF export for vet visits
  • Emergency Card and Medication Due widgets

Sirrius Pro

  • AI chat assistant — ask anything about your pet's health
  • Unlimited pets
  • Health score — a 0-100 snapshot of how up-to-date your pet's care is
  • Preventive care calendar — breed and age-aware schedules
  • Cost-of-skipping alerts — see what delayed care could cost
  • Symptom pattern analysis — spot trends across entries
  • Weight intelligence — flags when weight trends outside a healthy range for their breed
  • Home and Lock Screen widgets

Sirrius Pro is an auto-renewing subscription with a 7-day free trial on the annual plan. Manage or cancel anytime in your App Store settings.

Privacy

Your pet's records live on your devices and in your iCloud — not on our servers. Pro AI chat sends your question plus a short summary of the pet you're asking about (name, breed, age, medications, recent symptoms) through our relay to OpenAI to write the answer. We don't keep it. OpenAI may hold it up to 30 days for abuse checks, never for training, and it's never linked to you. Your photos and full records stay put. The safety checker runs entirely offline. No third-party analytics SDKs.
