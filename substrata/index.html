About Substrata

Wake up tired without knowing why? Headaches that hit out of nowhere? Substrata watches the noise, weather, and air around you, then pairs it with your sleep, heart rate, and HRV — so you can see exactly what's affecting how you feel.

It runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple Intelligence. After about two weeks, the on-device correlation engine starts surfacing connections — barometric pressure drops that match your migraine days, bedroom noise that quietly steals your deep sleep, weather patterns that drag down your HRV. Plain-English explanations you could share with your doctor.

Your sleep, explained

  • Sleep Condition Score: how well your bedroom actually treated you (noise, temperature, humidity, pressure stability)
  • Pairs with HealthKit sleep data + Apple Watch wrist temperature
  • Hourly, daily, weekly charts with WHO/EPA threshold zones color-coded

Headache & symptom triggers

  • Tap to log a headache (migraine, tension, cluster) with severity and notes
  • After 20-30 logged events, your personal trigger profile starts building
  • Predictive alerts when conditions match your past headache patterns
  • Also tracks allergies, mood, joint pain, energy

On-device intelligence

  • All correlation analysis runs on your iPhone
  • Microphone is not used in production; noise data comes from Apple Watch

What you get with Premium

  • Every correlation the engine finds (free tier shows your top one)
  • Personal headache trigger profile + predictive alerts
  • AI-generated insight narratives
  • PDF and CSV export — bring a real report to your doctor
  • History beyond 30 days

Requires iPhone with iOS 26 or later and an Apple Intelligence-capable device for AI insights. Apple Watch strongly recommended for environmental noise, HRV, and sleep-stage data; works without a Watch using weather, sleep duration, and headphone audio.

Substrata is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. It explores potential connections between environmental and health data for personal review. Consult a healthcare provider for diagnosis or treatment.

New in 1.7.0

Substrata 1.7 adds three new features and is a big reliability update.

  • New: widgets for your Home Screen and Lock Screen. Current Conditions shows noise level, pressure trend and an environmental summary; Quick Log records a headache in one tap, with Undo if it was a slip.
  • New: tap a prediction to see what matched, its track record for you, and something to try.
  • New: an optional weekly summary. Turn it on in Settings > Notifications, and a Sunday notification opens Your Week, seven days of your readings side by side.
  • Now fully translated into German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese and Brazilian Portuguese, including the doctor report.
  • Your whole history counts: patterns draw on every day you've tracked, not just the last 90, and Apple Watch data that syncs days late is still picked up.
  • A headache that runs past midnight stays on the Dashboard, and you can now set when it ended.
  • More careful statistics, and "Feeling OK" check-ins no longer count as headaches.
  • Face ID now covers every place your cycle and intimacy logs appear, including exports and share cards.
  • The doctor report includes your headache log, and CSV exports open cleanly in any spreadsheet.
  • Clearer VoiceOver labels, larger tap targets, a Dashboard that fits large text sizes and many smaller fixes.

Your data

  • Health, noise and sleep analysis runs on your iPhone; no audio is recorded
  • Weather comes from Apple WeatherKit, which receives your approximate location
