About Markki

Snap photos. Drop numbered callouts on every one. Markki keeps the numbers in sync across your whole report, even when you reshuffle photos.

One numbered legend, across every photo

Most apps restart the numbering on every photo, so you end up with three different "callout 1"s and a confused reader. Markki keeps callout 1 as callout 1 whether it's on photo 1 or photo 23, and builds one numbered legend on the PDF cover. Reshuffle photos? Numbers stay where you put them. Add a photo to slot 4 of 12? The legend updates itself.

How it works

  • Pick a template — Walk-through, Damage Report, Inventory, Punch List, or Compliance Audit
  • Snap photos (or pick from your library)
  • Tap a photo to drop a numbered callout. Type a label, or tap one of the template's suggestion chips.
  • Tap Export — Markki builds a multi-photo PDF with a cover, photos, and one numbered legend
  • Share via the iOS share sheet (email, AirDrop, Messages, Files)

Who it's for

Anyone who needs to send photo reports. Real estate walk-throughs, vehicle damage claims, contractor punch lists, equipment audits, classroom critiques, art portfolio markup, before-and-after shots, training materials, warehouse inventory checks, HOA compliance reviews. If you take photos and need to send numbered notes as a clean PDF, Markki turns thirty minutes of edit-and-renumber into five minutes of point-and-tap.
