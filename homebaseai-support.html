Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save items?
On any plan, tap + to add an item by hand (name, room, brand, model), or point your camera at a product label and HomeBaseAI fills in the model and serial numbers it can read. With HomeBase Pro, you can also just tell HomeBaseAI in conversation — type "I have a Samsung fridge in the kitchen" or tap the microphone and describe what you see — and the AI extracts the details and saves everything automatically.
What is the Home Health Score?
A number from 0 to 100 that shows how well-maintained your home is, based on maintenance compliance, overdue tasks, coverage, and documentation. It updates weekly. Available with HomeBase Pro.
How much does HomeBaseAI cost?
HomeBaseAI is free to download. Adding items, browsing, searching, label scanning, maintenance reminders, and family sharing are free. HomeBase Pro adds the AI assistant, home health score, seasonal checklists, cost-of-neglect tracking, and Pro widgets for $5.99/month or $49.99/year, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers (US prices; your App Store shows your local price). Everything you've saved stays yours and fully editable if your subscription ends.
How does Home Setup work?
Say "Home Setup" in the conversation and HomeBaseAI walks you through your major systems one by one — HVAC, water heater, appliances, safety equipment. Scan labels, skip what you don't have, pause and resume anytime. Maintenance reminders are created automatically.
How does the AI work?
HomeBase Pro uses OpenAI's GPT-4o-mini model for conversation. When a Pro subscriber sends a message, it goes over HTTPS through a small relay server we operate (on Cloudflare) to OpenAI, along with the relevant items from your home inventory. The relay does not store or log your messages. OpenAI does not use them for training and keeps them for at most 30 days for abuse monitoring, then deletes them. Without Pro, messages are answered on your device by keyword search and are never sent anywhere. Label reading (OCR) happens on your device; only the recognized text is sent, never the photo. Voice input is transcribed on your device where your iPhone and language support it; otherwise Apple's speech recognition service transcribes it.
Does HomeBaseAI work offline?
Your vault data (items, materials, rooms) is always accessible offline. AI conversation requires an internet connection. If you're offline, you can still browse your saved items and detail views.
Is my data private?
Your home data is stored on your device and in your private iCloud. Pro conversation messages, with the relevant inventory items, pass through our relay to OpenAI for processing; the relay doesn't keep them, and OpenAI doesn't use them for training and deletes them within 30 days. To confirm Pro access, the relay keeps a hashed subscription reference for up to 48 hours. We have no accounts, no third-party analytics SDKs, no third-party tracking, and no ads. Paywall flow diagnostics use Apple-native instrumentation only (OSLog and MetricKit signposts) that stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
How does family sharing work?
You can invite family members to share a home via iCloud. Everyone can see and update the same items, rooms, and maintenance tasks. Sharing uses Apple's CloudKit — data syncs through your iCloud accounts, not through our servers.
What happens if I delete the app?
All local data is permanently removed. Shared data in iCloud persists for other family members who still have the app. We don't hold a copy of your home data: our AI relay doesn't store your messages, and its hashed subscription reference expires on its own.
How do I manage my subscription?
Go to Settings on your iPhone > your Apple ID > Subscriptions > HomeBase Pro. You can also tap "Manage Subscription" in the app's Settings screen.
What iOS version is required?
HomeBaseAI requires iOS 17 or later.
