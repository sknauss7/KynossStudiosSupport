Privacy

{{ site.studio.legal }} ("we") builds custom software for businesses and makes iPhone apps. We collect as little as we can, we say here what we do collect, and we never sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Writing to us or booking a call

When you write to us through a form on this site (on our home page, our custom software page, or a page an ad of ours leads to), we receive your name, the email or phone number you give, your business name if you add it, your message, and which page you wrote from. We use them only to reply to you and to talk about working together. We don't sell them or share them with anyone for marketing.

When you book a call with us through a booking link we sent you, we receive your name, email and phone number and the time you chose, and use them only to hold and confirm that call.

The form and the booking page are handled by a small service we run on Cloudflare, Inc. A form message waits there until we move it into our own private records, usually within a day, and is then deleted from Cloudflare. We don't keep your IP address with your message; it's used only for one hour, in a scrambled form, to stop the form being flooded. In our records, AI models from Anthropic PBC (Claude) read your message to help us sort it and draft a reply.

We keep form messages and booking details for one year after our last contact with you, then delete them. Ask us at any time to delete what you sent: {{ site.studio.email }}.

When you email us

Email to our addresses is held for us by Google LLC (Google Workspace). AI models from Anthropic PBC (Claude) help us read and sort it, flag what needs an answer, and draft replies, which a person approves before they are sent. We keep email in our own private records for one year after our last contact with you, longer only where the law requires it, as for invoices and tax records. Ask us at any time to see or delete yours: {{ site.studio.email }}.

If we emailed your business

We sometimes email a small business we think we could help. We find the business in public listings, such as state business records and map listings, and on its own website, and we use the contact address it publishes. We keep a short record of the business: its name, its public contact details, what its website shows about how it works, the emails we sent, and whether it replied.

We send at most two emails. Each one says how to stop them: reply "no thanks" and we won't email you again. We keep your address and that answer so that we can honour it. Ask us to delete the rest of the record at any time: {{ site.studio.email }}.

Example pages we make for businesses

Sometimes we make a private example page for a business we'd like to work with, at demo.kynossstudios.com. It uses only public information about that business. It isn't linked from this site, it's hidden from search engines, and we take it down after a set time.

When an example page is opened, we record that visit: which page, the time, and whether it was opened on a phone or a computer. We also record what is pressed on the page and when: play, pause, skip, back and replay, the call and email buttons, when the video starts and ends, and which slide was reached. We don't use cookies, we don't keep IP addresses, and nothing in the record names the visitor. Each example page is made for one business, though, so a visit to it tells us someone opened that business's page. The record is kept for 180 days.

A few pages we make for a business we're already talking with ask questions. Answers sent from such a page are kept on the service for 90 days and in our records of that business.

The pages run on Cloudflare, Inc. Ask us to take a page down, or to delete its record, at any time: {{ site.studio.email }}.

Visit counts on this site

Four kinds of page on this site count visits: the pages our ads open (kynossstudios.com/owners/), our home page, our custom software page, and the page our business cards open (kynossstudios.com/card/). Pandemoni sticker pages are counted separately, as described under our apps below. No other page on this site is counted.

For each visit we record which page, the time, whether it was opened on a phone or a computer, whether the visitor came from an ad (a yes or no; we never keep the ad's click code), which buttons were tapped, how far down the page was scrolled, and how many seconds passed before the first tap. The card page records less: the time, phone or computer, which buttons were tapped, and the seconds before the first tap.

Each visit gets a random four-character label that exists only while the page is open, so we can't tell visitors apart or link two visits. We don't use cookies and we don't keep IP addresses, and we skip visitors whose browser says Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Pressing a button is counted; what you type in our form is never part of the count. Our pages carry no advertising company's tracking code, so an ad company learns nothing from us about what you do after a click.

The count runs on Cloudflare, Inc. and is kept for 180 days. Because nothing in the count identifies you, we can't pick out your visits. Ask us and we'll delete the count for the page or day you name: {{ site.studio.email }}.

Calls and texts to our business line

When you call or text our business number, we receive your phone number, the time, and what you say or write. If you leave a message, we also receive what you tell us about yourself and your business. We use this only to answer you, to get back to you, to check and improve how our business line answers, and, if you're a client, to support the system we built for you.

An AI manager answers our calls. It tells you so at the start of the call, along with a notice that the call is transcribed. It can answer questions about our services, take a message, or put your call through to our team. We keep a written transcript of each call, including the part after we put you through, but never the audio. Texts may be translated by AI so that we can read and reply in your language; we keep the original alongside the translation.

We review how our line handles each call and text. After each one, an AI model checks it against our own rules for the line. We keep that review for one year, and it holds no phone number or name. When a text comes from a number we don't know, an AI model also sorts it (for example, a person, spam or a wrong number), so that the people who need a reply reach us quickly. We keep every text, whatever the sort.

Spam calls. When a caller we don't know opens with a recorded sales pitch, our line keeps a scrambled fingerprint of the opening words for 30 days, so the same recording is recognised and ended sooner next time. The words themselves are not kept in that list. A number that sends us spam twice in 30 days is turned away for 180 days; we keep it only in scrambled form.

Notes for planning. For calls with businesses and partners we're working with, an AI model also writes notes from the transcript (what was asked, the needs mentioned and any follow-ups), so we can plan and build what we discussed. We keep those notes with our records of that business and never use them for advertising.

Four service providers handle calls and texts for us, only to provide this service to us:

  • Twilio Inc., which carries the calls and texts and turns speech into text;
  • Anthropic PBC, whose Claude models answer, summarize, sort, review and translate;
  • Cloudflare, Inc., which runs the small service that connects them;
  • Slack Technologies, LLC, where our team is alerted to a new call or text. Phone numbers and other long numbers are hidden in those alerts.

The messages and transcripts are then kept in our own private records.

We keep calls and texts for one year after our last contact with you, then delete them. If you become a client, your service agreement sets how long we keep them instead.

Texts: message frequency varies with the conversation. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts from us, and HELP for help. Our text messaging terms have the details.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party. We don't sell your information.

Ask us at any time to see or delete your calls and texts: {{ site.studio.email }}.

If we build a system for your business

Your customers' details stay yours.

  • Your own private system. Each business gets its own system with its own encrypted storage. Your records are never mixed with another business's. It runs on a computer at your business or on a server we rent for you alone; your agreement says which. When we host your system, it sits on a server in our account, and we don't open your records unless you ask us to help.
  • Who sees your records. You, and the people you choose. We see how your system is doing (that its checks pass, and counts like how many texts went out or how often something failed), never your customers' names, numbers or messages, unless you write to us about one or ask us to help. Feedback you write to us is kept for one year. Calls, website chats and form messages can wait, sealed, with the service that passes them to your system for up to 7 days; we don't read them.
  • The AI. It gets only what each task needs: the message or call it is answering and the facts for the answer. Door and gate codes we detect in texts are taken out first, and customers' phone numbers from your records aren't sent. The AI company doesn't train its AI on your data and deletes it within 30 days.
  • Texts. Deleted from the texting service once your system has saved them or they've been delivered.
  • Backups. Every night, encrypted before they leave your system, so the backup company can't read them.
  • Keys we keep a copy of. Only the ones you agree to, so we can restore your system if it's ever lost. Every use is logged, and if we use one to restore your system or because you asked, you're told within one business day.
  • If you leave. You get all your data in a standard export, and then we delete our copies within 30 days. Encrypted backups age out within a year.
  • Your customers' choices. A customer who asks to be forgotten is deleted with a tap, and a customer who texts STOP gets no more texts. Encrypted backups age out within a year.

Your service agreement and data agreement set this out in full.
