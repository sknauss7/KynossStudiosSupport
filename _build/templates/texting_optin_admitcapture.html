How to sign up for AdmitCapture outage notices

{{ site.studio.legal }} · Texts from {{ alerts.phone }}

{{ site.studio.legal }} runs AdmitCapture, the admissions paging system of a hospitalist group. When AdmitCapture isn't reaching phones, we text the people who signed up, and text again when it's working. There are two ways to sign up, shown below. Nobody gets these texts without signing up, and signing up is never a condition of anything. The full terms are in section 11 of our text messaging terms.

1. Doctors: a checkbox at AdmitCapture sign-up

When a doctor signs up for AdmitCapture, the sign-up screen shows this box. It starts unticked, and the doctor can sign up without ticking it.

I agree that AdmitCapture may call and text my phone, including automated calls and texts, about admission requests, messages and my shifts, and that Kynoss Studios, which runs AdmitCapture, may text me when it isn't working and when it's back. I can turn texts off by replying STOP.

The wording as it appears on the sign-up screen.

2. Emergency department contacts: text JOIN

Each emergency department's AdmitCapture page, and a printed card at the department's desk, shows this:

Get a text if AdmitCapture stops working. Text JOIN to {{ alerts.phone }}.

Kynoss Studios texts you when AdmitCapture isn't reaching phones, and again when it's back. A few texts a month at most. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop, HELP for help. Terms: kynossstudios.com/texting-terms. Privacy: kynossstudios.com/privacy.

After you text JOIN, you get one reply:

Kynoss Studios: you're signed up for AdmitCapture outage notices, a few texts a month at most. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to stop.

Stopping, help and privacy

Reply STOP at any time to stop, and HELP for help. You can also email {{ site.studio.email }} or call {{ site.studio.phone }}. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party. See our privacy page.
