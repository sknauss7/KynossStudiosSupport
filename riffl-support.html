Riffl

Unshuffle two words. One puzzle a day.

How to Play

Each day, two words are shuffled together into a single row of letters. Each word's letters stay in the order you'd read them. Assign every letter to the blue word or the orange word to split them back into the two correct words. On Challenge Mondays, three words are shuffled together.

  • Drag a letter to a word tray to assign it, or tap a letter and then tap a tray.
  • Once placed, a letter is locked in.
  • Made a wrong call? Tap Clear Board to reset all letters.
  • Need a nudge? Tap Hint to reveal one letter.
  • When every letter is assigned and the words are correct, you win!

Scoring

Your score is the number of lives you used. Each Clear Board and each Hint costs one life, and you never run out. Zero lives used means you nailed it on the first try.

Daily Puzzle

Everyone gets the same puzzle each day. A new puzzle is available every day at midnight (your local time). Your progress is saved automatically if you leave mid-game.

Streaks & Stats

Riffl tracks your current streak, longest streak, games played, and how many lives you used — all stored locally on your device. If you're signed in to Game Center, you can also compare daily scores and streaks on the leaderboards (free for everyone).

Sharing Results

After solving, you can share your result as a spoiler-free emoji card. No words are revealed — just your lives used and a blue-and-orange pattern showing how you split the letters.

Riffl Pro

The daily puzzle is free. Riffl Pro is an optional one-time purchase of $2.99 (US price; your App Store shows your local price) — no subscription. It unlocks Practice Mode (unlimited random puzzles), the Puzzle Archive, Detailed Stats, and Visual Themes. Already bought it on another device? Use Restore Purchase in Settings.

Accessibility

Riffl supports VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, and Reduce Motion. If you encounter any accessibility issues, please reach out.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?
No. There are no accounts, logins, or sign-ups.

Does Riffl work offline?
Yes. Puzzles, stats, and streaks work entirely offline. A connection is only needed for Game Center leaderboards and for buying or restoring Riffl Pro.

Can I get hints?
Yes. Tap Hint to reveal one letter. Each hint costs one life, so your score reflects it, but you never run out. Every puzzle is solvable — take your time!

How do I reset my stats?
Deleting and reinstalling the app will reset all data.
