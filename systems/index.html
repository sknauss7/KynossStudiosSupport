AI-built. Not AI slop.

Software thrown together with AI has earned a reputation for looking fine and falling apart. Ours goes through strict testing and review before it reaches you, because the experience is the product.

  • Seen working, not just “tests pass”

    Nothing counts as done until it has been run and checked on screen. Passing tests alone don't prove it works.

  • Judged on how it feels

    Independent AI reviewers look at every screen the way a newcomer would: is it clear at a glance, and would you show it to a friend? It doesn't ship without a pass.

  • Tested in real conditions

    Automated tests run against real databases, real browsers and simulated iPhones, and they run again on every change.

  • Reviewed before every release

    The code is reviewed line by line, the way a careful colleague would, before anything goes out.

  • A failed check stays failed

    When something misses its target, nobody moves the goalposts or calls it close enough. It gets fixed.

  • Known issues get fixed, not shipped

    Anything spotted before launch is fixed first, even small cosmetic things.

How we'd build yours

The same way we built ours: start from the idea, build around how you actually work, and keep improving it once it's running.

  1. Tell us the idea

    A sentence is enough: the thing you keep losing track of, the job that eats your evenings, the question you wish you could just ask.

  2. We map how the work happens

    Where information arrives, what decisions you make, who else is involved and what a great result looks like to you.

  3. You decide what it handles

    What it does on its own, what it checks with you first and how it reaches you. It's your system, set up your way.

  4. We build it and switch it on

    You see it working on your real day-to-day, then it grows one piece at a time as your needs do.

Questions people ask

What can it connect to?

Whatever your work already runs on: email, texts, calendars, shared folders, spreadsheets, websites and many online services.

Does it have to be an app?

No. It can be a message on your phone each morning, a website, a private dashboard, an iPhone app, or a mix of them.

How much does it do on its own?

As much or as little as you like. Some people want drafts to approve; others want jobs handled start to finish. You choose, and you can change it later.

Where does my information live?

We agree on that up front and set it up the way you're comfortable with.

What does it cost?

It depends on what the system does. Tell us the idea and we'll come back with a plan and a price.

What happens once it's built?

It keeps working for you, and we can keep extending it as your needs change.
