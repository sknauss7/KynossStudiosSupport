About Tylde

Tylde is async word PvP for friends — share a 5×5 board, race a 90-second timer, meet at the reveal.

Open the day's match when you have 90 seconds. Build words from the same shared board everyone is racing. Submit. Then wait.

At the reveal, everyone's words go live at once. Tile-claim resolution decides who scored which letters — longest word wins the tile, rare words break ties, first submission settles it. Get the receipts: your rarest word played, the tiles you uniquely claimed, your final placement.

— Friend matches: 1v1 or 3-8 with your Game Center friends. — Day-1 personal-best bot mode: jump in immediately, no waiting for friends. Bots are clearly labeled — never disguised as humans. — Cross-suite cards: pull from Riffl, Paeki, Intuii, Rusti when you're in the mood for a different daily puzzle.

Pro unlock: archive of every match, detailed stats, themed share artifacts, multi-round friend tournaments. One-time purchase, no subscription.

No accounts. No ads. No tracking. Game Center identity, on-device privacy, your match data stays yours.

New in 1.1.0

Friend matches work end to end. After your turn, Tylde takes you straight to the waiting screen, and if your words didn't send, it says so and tries again. Home always has a way to start a new match, new invitations keep arriving however many games you've played, and leaving mid-turn locks in your words instead of pausing the clock.

Reveals you can trust. A result is final once everyone has played or the 24 hours are up. Until then you'll see early standings, clearly marked.

Rare words are real now. Every word is ranked by how often people actually use it, so rare words break ties, earn their bonus, and get a gold star at the reveal.

Tylde Pro: detailed stats now show win rate by mode, rare-word rate, longest path and average claim ratio. Tournaments finish when their last round does.

Also: group invites for any number of friends, clearer notification settings, better support for larger text, haptics on the board, and many smaller fixes.
