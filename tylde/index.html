About Tylde

Tylde is async word PvP for friends — share a 5×5 board, race a 90-second timer, meet at the reveal.

Open the day's match when you have 90 seconds. Build words from the same shared board everyone is racing. Submit. Then wait.

At the reveal, everyone's words go live at once. Tile-claim resolution decides who scored which letters — longest word wins the tile, rare words break ties, first submission settles it. Get the receipts: your rarest word played, the tiles you uniquely claimed, your final placement.

— Friend matches: 1v1 or 3-8 with your Game Center friends. — Day-1 personal-best bot mode: jump in immediately, no waiting for friends. Bots are clearly labeled — never disguised as humans. — Cross-suite cards: pull from Riffl, Paeki, Intuii, Rusti when you're in the mood for a different daily puzzle.

Pro unlock: archive of every match, detailed stats, themed share artifacts, multi-round friend tournaments. One-time purchase, no subscription.

New in 1.2.0

Bot matches now always show the real result: your score, the bot's score and who won.

Designed for iPhone Duo's inner display: the board sits on one half and your timer, words and scores on the other, with nothing on the fold. Reveals spread out the same way, and folding or unfolding mid-match keeps your turn and the clock running.

Also: fixes for iOS 27, a board that stays square and the same size as you play, taps that land on the tile under your finger, close buttons on sheets, and better layouts at large text sizes.
