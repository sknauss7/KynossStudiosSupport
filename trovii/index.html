About Trovii

Save your children's artwork together in one family timeline, then turn each year into a beautiful photobook.

Trovii makes it effortless for parents of multiple kids to capture, organize, and preserve every drawing, painting, and craft your children make — without spreadsheets, paper bins, or yet another cloud account.

Why parents with multiple children love Trovii

  • ONE FAMILY TIMELINE — see all your kids' artwork together, in chronological order. Filter to one kid when you want to. No more switching between separate per-kid folders.
  • SNAP AT THE FRIDGE — open the camera, snap, done. Trovii's on-device AI auto-crops the artwork from the surface (table, fridge, floor) so you don't have to. Single-handed, no signup.
  • FAMILY DIGEST — every month, Trovii prepares a 5-card digest of the family's best new artwork. Share it with family through Apple's secure family-sharing — they see it on their iPhone, no app to install.
  • YEAR-END PHOTOBOOK — at the end of every year, export a beautiful multi-kid photobook PDF. Share it, print it through any photo service, or save it as a digital keepsake.
  • POLISHED, NOT PLAYFUL — Trovii is designed for parents who want a calm, gallery-style archive of their kids' creativity, not another chaotic photo app.

Pro membership

A 3-day free trial unlocks unlimited captures, sibling timelines, family digests, and year-end photobook export. Continue with monthly, yearly, or one-time lifetime — your choice. No upsells inside the app.
