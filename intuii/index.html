About Intuii

Most games test what you know. Intuii tests how well you know what you don't.

Five estimation questions drop every day. How many piano tuners are in Chicago? How far is it to the Moon? You won't know the exact answer — nobody does. That's the point.

Enter your best guess, then drag out a range you'd bet 90% on. Too narrow and you'll miss. Too wide and you'll pay for playing it safe. Your confidence score reveals the gap between what you think you know and what you actually know.

Every player gets the same five questions each day. Track your streak and share your daily result card.

Everyone gets

  • Five fresh questions daily across 15 categories
  • A score that rewards honesty about what you don't know
  • Daily streaks
  • Shareable result cards

Upgrade to Pro

  • Full archive of past puzzles
  • Practice mode with 4,000+ questions
  • On-device charts track your accuracy by category and over time — no account, no cloud
  • Streak freeze (one per week)

All data stays on your device. No accounts. No tracking. Just you and your intuition.

New in 1.3.2

A fairness-and-polish update for your daily five.

  • Honest ranges now win. Tighter ranges pay more when they land, and Pro Stats show how often yours really do.
  • Game Center leaderboards are live: a fresh daily score board every day, plus your best streak.
  • Questions whose answers were too big or too small to type are rewritten in units you can enter, so every question in the bank is back in play.
  • Achievements now tell you the moment you earn one.
  • Easier-to-grab range handles, and better support for VoiceOver, larger text and Reduce Motion.
  • Left on the last question before tapping Next? Your day now counts when you come back, instead of hanging on "Loading".
  • Home shows today's puzzle as solved the moment you finish, wherever you live.
  • Answers under 1 now score correctly.
  • The reveal now states the answer, its unit and its source.
  • Your streak on Home and Stats now reflects missed days.
  • Archive and results show the puzzle's own date, and practice rounds no longer share as today's puzzle.
  • Rate Intuii opens the App Store review page, and Delete All Data also cancels your daily reminder.
