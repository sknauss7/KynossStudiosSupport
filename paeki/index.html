About Paeki

One hidden picture. Sixteen frosted tiles. How sharp is your eye?

Every day Paeki gives you a fresh image hidden beneath a grid of frosted glass tiles. Tap a tile to reveal a piece of the picture, then type your guess. The fewer tiles you need, the higher your score. It understands what you mean — synonyms, close answers, and common variations all count.

How it works

  • A new puzzle every day at midnight — same image for everyone
  • Each tile you reveal costs points — guess early for a better score
  • Use hints if you're stuck — category hint or a detailed clue
  • Share your result as an emoji grid without spoiling the answer

Compete with friends

  • Game Center leaderboard — daily rankings by score (fewer tiles, higher score)
  • 10 achievements to unlock: streaks, speed solves, perfect scores, and more
  • Share your emoji grid and challenge friends to beat your tile count

PAEKI PRO (one-time purchase)

  • Puzzle Archive — revisit every past puzzle and relive your best solves
  • Weekly Challenge — 7 themed puzzles a week with a cumulative score
  • Nerd out on your stats — solve rate, average score, streaks, and trends
  • Practice Mode — unlimited puzzles filtered by category and difficulty
  • Ten visual themes to make the game yours

New in 1.4.0

Fairer, steadier, easier to read.

  • Fairer answers: near-misses no longer sneak through, and more right answers count, especially on devices without Apple Intelligence.
  • Every puzzle re-checked: answers and hints now match their pictures, and photos that didn't make fair puzzles are gone.
  • Pro: replay any puzzle from the Archive.
  • Practice and the Weekly Challenge never spoil today's daily or one that's coming.
  • Giving up or hitting midnight mid-guess no longer scrambles your score. Solve times are right, and a hint you paid for stays revealed.
  • Widgets flip to the new day on time and stay readable in Light Mode and at large text sizes.
  • Accessibility: the result card scrolls at large text sizes, VoiceOver labels are clearer, and Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency are respected.
  • Delete All Data now really clears everything, reminders included.

Your data

  • Puzzles, guesses and stats stay on your iPhone, including the on-device AI that checks guesses
  • Leaderboards use Game Center
