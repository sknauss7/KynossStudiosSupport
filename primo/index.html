About Primo

Primo counts backward from every occasion to build a personalized action plan — calibrated to how much each relationship means to you.

Add the people you care about, set their important dates, and Primo does the rest. Its reverse-timeline engine counts backward from each event to generate a detailed plan: when to start brainstorming, when to buy the gift, when to make the reservation, and when to send the message.

Key features

  • Reverse-Timeline Planning

Every occasion gets a step-by-step action plan that counts backward from the date. Items are grouped by week with due dates, categories, and priorities so nothing falls through the cracks.

  • 4 Levels of Effort

Not every relationship needs the same effort. Pick All Out, Thoughtful, Simple Gesture, or Acknowledge — Primo starts reminders earlier and builds a deeper plan for the people who matter most.

  • AI-Powered Gift Suggestions

Get personalized gift ideas powered by Apple Intelligence, running entirely on your device. Primo considers interests, relationship type, past gifts, and what worked before. Rate suggestions to improve future recommendations.

  • Message Drafting

Draft personalized messages for any occasion with tone and length controls. Copy or share directly from the app.

  • Gift Tracking & Insights

Log every gift with cost and reaction. See what landed, spot patterns across years, and gift smarter next time.

  • Budget Tracking

Set a budget for any occasion and watch spending add up — see what's left before you overspend.

  • Smart Calendar Import

Import birthdays, anniversaries, graduations, and more from your device calendar. Primo recognizes event types and even figures out shared occasions like "Amy and Jim's anniversary."

  • Holiday Manager

Select which holidays matter to you. Primo creates occasions and starts the plan weeks before each one.

  • Notifications That Help

Reminders tuned to how much each person matters — from a 2-day heads-up for your partner's birthday to a morning-of nudge for a coworker's.

  • Share Your Plans

Share occasion plans as a text checklist or celebration card image.

  • Backup & Restore

Export your data to a file and import it anywhere. Your data stays yours.

New in 2.2.0

Primo opens up on iPhone Duo.

  • Unfold and Plans spreads out: your upcoming occasions on one side, the plan you pick on the other — with its next step, Mark Done and notes right there.
  • Fold or unfold mid-plan and Primo keeps your place.
  • Home shows what's up next, and People, Settings and Gift History read in a comfortable column on the wide screen.
  • Fixes for iOS 27: the API key setup, idea editing, plan review and backup sharing screens open with the right content every time.
  • Tasks and occasions on the same day keep their order, and the occasion summary fits smaller screens.
