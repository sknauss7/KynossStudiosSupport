About HomeBaseAI

HomeBaseAI is the simplest way to manage everything about your home. Just have a conversation.

Your home, in one conversation

Add what you have in a few taps -- name, room, brand, model -- or, with Pro, just tell HomeBaseAI by typing, speaking, or snapping a photo and it handles the rest. Browse everything room by room in My Home, or ask where something is.

With Pro, say "I just bought a Samsung fridge for the kitchen" and HomeBaseAI saves it instantly with a confirmation card. It also auto-creates maintenance reminders based on what you saved -- water filter every 6 months, condenser coils every 6 months -- so you never forget.

Snap a label

Point your camera at any product label, paint can, or appliance tag. On any plan, HomeBaseAI attaches the photo and fills in the model and serial numbers it can read. With Pro, AI reads the text and extracts every detail -- brand, model number, serial number, color code -- then saves it to the right room automatically.

A coach that knows your home

HomeBaseAI doesn't just store data -- it uses it. Ask "My water heater is making a popping sound" and get a personalized diagnosis based on your actual water heater's age and maintenance history. Ask "What should I do before winter?" and get a checklist built from your actual inventory, not generic advice.

Home health score

One number that tells you if you're on top of your home -- or if things are slipping. HomeBaseAI watches maintenance compliance, overdue tasks, coverage, and documentation completeness and rolls them into a single 0-100 score. Watch it climb week by week as you stay ahead.

Seasonal prep checklists

Every season, HomeBaseAI generates a prep checklist based on what you actually own. No sprinkler system? No sprinkler winterization task. Have a gas furnace? Chimney inspection appears in fall.

Cost-of-neglect tracking

See the real cost of deferred maintenance. "Your dryer vent cleaning is 4 months overdue. Lint buildup increases fire risk and costs roughly $8/month in dryer efficiency. Total accrued: ~$32."

Widgets

See overdue tasks, your next maintenance item, and your home health score right on your Lock Screen and Home Screen.

Shared with family

Share your home with family through iCloud. Everyone sees the same data, everyone can contribute. No accounts needed.

Guided home setup

Say "Home Setup" and walk through your major systems one by one -- HVAC, water heater, appliances, safety equipment. Scan labels, skip what you don't have, pause and resume anytime. Maintenance reminders are created automatically as you go.

Free, with an optional Pro

Adding, browsing, searching, maintenance reminders, and family sharing are free. HomeBase Pro adds the AI assistant, home health score, seasonal checklists, cost-of-neglect tracking, and Pro widgets: $5.99/month or $49.99/year (save 30%), with a free trial for new subscribers. Cancel anytime. Everything you've saved stays yours -- fully editable -- even if your subscription ends.

New in 2.3.0

Designed for iPhone Duo's inner display: ask about something you saved, and the item or material the answer came from opens right beside the conversation, one on each side of the fold. The welcome and setup screens fit the wide display too.

  • Answers now name the saved item they came from. Tap it to open it.
  • The button to get started is always on screen during setup, even on smaller screens and with larger text.
  • Home Setup picks up where you left off after a move, a reinstall or on a new phone.
  • Smoke and CO detectors saved in Home Setup now get their maintenance reminders.
  • Widgets and the subscription screen show the home you're working in now.
  • Fixes for iOS 27, including item details that showed a large blank gap.

Your data

  • Your home records sync through your private iCloud, and share by invite
  • Pro AI chat sends your question and related home details through our relay to OpenAI; the relay doesn't store it, OpenAI may keep it up to 30 days for abuse checks and never trains on it
