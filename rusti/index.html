About Rusti

Each morning, ten questions on the basics every adult learned — and most have forgotten.

Multiplying fractions. World capitals. Body systems. Vocabulary roots. The stuff that was easy at ten and hits different at thirty-five.

Play the daily ritual solo, climb the friend leaderboard, or get pulled into a household showdown with the kids. Then challenge a friend head-to-head on a single subtopic — multiplying fractions, world capitals, parts of speech — and see who's actually rusty.

In the daily ritual:

  • Ten questions per day, sixty seconds each
  • Single attempt — share your spoiler-safe scorecard at any time
  • Friend leaderboard via Game Center
  • Family household leaderboard shared through iCloud, with kid sub-accounts that stay COPPA-clean by construction

Unlocks with Rusti+:

  • Practice Mode — unlimited practice quizzes that start with questions you've already seen and won't preview your next month of dailies
  • Family Dashboard — parent-facing weekly per-kid report
  • Challenge Mode — initiate head-to-head challenges on any subtopic, accuracy plus speed scoring
  • Plus replays of any quiz you've played, detailed per-subject stats with your current and longest streaks, your score trend and consistency, a monthly streak freeze, themed share artifacts, a reminder at the time you choose, and unlimited kid sub-accounts

Built by Kynoss Studios. Daily Games suite member alongside Riffl, Paeki, and Intuii.

New in 1.2.0

v1.2.0 — A fairer daily, and a lot of fixes

We went through Rusti end to end. Here's what changed.

The daily

  • Answers are shuffled now, so the right one isn't usually A.
  • One attempt means one attempt. Closing the app mid-quiz or opening a challenge no longer gives you a fresh start.
  • We re-checked all 3,785 questions and rewrote over a thousand: wrong answer keys, repeats, muddy wording, and trivia no school ever taught.
  • Every math question now works in your head. No paper, no calculator.
  • The daily reminder works again. Newer installs weren't getting it.
  • Every screen now follows your text size setting.

Challenges

  • Challenge Mode opens reliably again, and a challenge link takes you straight to that challenge.
  • Challenge specific Game Center friends, then play your own challenge alongside them.

Family

  • Kids can play the daily on your iPhone and land on the family board. Tap Today's Quiz and pick who's playing.
  • You can leave a household, or delete one you created.

Rusti+

  • Replay any daily you've played, from the Archive.
  • Calibration shows your score trend and how consistent you are.
  • Stats has a streak card: current streak and longest.

Still ten questions. Still the basics.

Your data

  • Progress syncs through your private iCloud
  • Friend challenges and household leaderboards go through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit)
  • Game Center for your player identity
