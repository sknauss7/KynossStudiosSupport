1. Overview

Widgii ("the App") is developed and published by Kynoss Studios ("we," "us," or "our"). Widgii is a single-board production puzzle for iPhone (iOS 26 and later) in which you route material across a grid to meet an output demand. All gameplay runs on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains what data Widgii accesses, how that data is processed, and your rights regarding your information. We are committed to transparency and to protecting your privacy.

Widgii operates entirely on your device. We do not operate servers, databases, or cloud services. We do not create user accounts. The App makes no network requests. We have no mechanism to collect, receive, view, or store your personal data. The data Widgii works with exists solely on your iPhone, under your control.

2. Your Game Data

Your game data stays on your device. Which boards you have solved, your best score on each, and your settings are stored locally. None of it is transmitted to Kynoss Studios or to any third party.

What Widgii Stores Locally

Data Stored Purpose Shared Externally?
Per-board progress (solved or not, first solved date) Track which boards you have completed No
Your best elegance score per board Show your personal best and let you try to beat it No
In-progress board layout Restore your line if you leave mid-board No
App settings and preferences Remember how you like the app configured No
Purchase status, if the App offers an in-app purchase Unlock purchased content Apple (StoreKit) manages transactions

Widgii does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, health data, or any other personal information, and has no screen on which to enter any.

3. Data Storage & On-Device Processing

All processing occurs entirely on your iPhone. Widgii has no servers, no cloud infrastructure, no databases, and no backend of any kind. We literally cannot access your data because we have no system to receive it.

Game data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework, configured for on-device storage only with no iCloud synchronization. This data is protected by:

  • iOS Data Protection (encrypted at rest, tied to your device passcode)
  • App sandboxing (only Widgii can access its own data store)
  • Standard iOS security architecture

Widgii does not use:

  • iCloud, CloudKit, or any cloud storage or sync
  • Any third-party SDKs, frameworks, or libraries
  • Analytics services (no Firebase, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude, etc.)
  • Advertising SDKs or ad networks
  • Crash reporting services
  • Push notifications
  • Game Center, leaderboards, accounts, or social sign-in
  • Cloud or networked AI services
  • Any form of user tracking or fingerprinting

The simulation that runs each board — belts, machines, delivery rates, scoring — is computed on your device and nowhere else.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Retention Policy

  • Your progress, scores, and settings are retained on your device until you delete the app.
  • Widgii does not auto-prune your history.

Deletion

  • Delete the app: Deleting Widgii from your device permanently removes all local data, including your progress and scores.
  • There is no server-side data to delete, no account to close, and no request process needed. You have full and immediate control.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Widgii does not share your data with anyone.

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your data with third parties.
  • We do not transmit your data off your device.
  • We do not use third-party SDKs that could collect data.
  • We do not serve advertisements.
  • We do not engage in data mining or user profiling.
  • We do not share data with parent companies, subsidiaries, or related entities (Kynoss Studios is an independent sole developer).

6. Data Security

Widgii relies on Apple's built-in security infrastructure to protect your data:

  • Encryption at rest: All local data is protected by iOS Data Protection, which encrypts data using keys tied to your device passcode.
  • App sandboxing: iOS prevents other apps from accessing Widgii's data store.
  • No network exposure: Because Widgii does not transmit data over any network, there is no risk of data interception, server breaches, or unauthorized remote access.
  • No accounts: There are no credentials to steal, no passwords to breach, and no accounts to compromise.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Widgii is rated 4+ for general suitability. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Because Widgii does not collect, transmit, or store personal information on any server, and because all data remains solely on the user's device, Widgii does not create the data collection risks that COPPA is designed to address. Nonetheless:

  • Widgii does not collect personal information from any user, including children.
  • Widgii does not require or support account creation.
  • Widgii contains no advertising, social features, or in-app communication.
  • Widgii does not transmit any data to Kynoss Studios or any third party.

If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about this policy, please contact us at kynossstudios@gmail.com.

8. Your Rights Under GDPR (European Economic Area)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) provides you with specific rights regarding your personal data. We respect these rights, even though Widgii's on-device architecture means we do not act as a data controller or processor of your personal data in the traditional sense — because your data never reaches us.

Legal Basis for Processing

To the extent any on-device processing constitutes "processing" under the GDPR, the legal basis is:

  • Legitimate interest: Processing your data on your own device to provide the app's gameplay and progress tracking directly to you.

Your Rights

  • Right of access: All data is stored on your device and visible to you within the app.
  • Right to erasure: You can delete all data by deleting the app.
  • Right to restrict processing: Existing data is not used for any purpose beyond your own play.
  • Right to data portability: We hold none of your data, so there is nothing for us to port. Your data lives only on your device, under your control.
  • Right to object: You can stop all data processing by deleting the app.

Data Transfers

Widgii does not transfer personal data outside your device. No cross-border data transfers by Kynoss Studios occur.

Data Protection Officer

Due to the nature of our data processing (entirely on-device, no data collection), we are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer. For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at kynossstudios@gmail.com.

9. Your Rights Under CCPA/CPRA (California)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) provides you with specific rights.

Information We Collect

Under CCPA/CPRA definitions, Widgii processes the following categories of information on your device only:

  • Board progress, scores, and app settings

However, because all processing occurs on your device and we never collect, receive, or access this information, Kynoss Studios does not "collect" personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

Your Rights

  • Right to know: This Privacy Policy describes all data the app processes. We do not collect additional data beyond what is described here.
  • Right to delete: Delete the app to remove all local data.
  • Right to opt out of sale: We do not sell your personal information. We never have and never will.
  • Right to opt out of sharing: We do not share your personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising or any other purpose.
  • Right to non-discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.

"Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information"

Widgii does not sell or share personal information. Because no data is transmitted from your device to us or any third party, there is no sale or sharing to opt out of. We fully comply with the CCPA/CPRA without requiring any user action.

10. Apple Platform Disclosures

In compliance with Apple's App Store Review Guidelines and platform requirements:

In-App Purchases

  • Widgii uses no payment processing of its own. Any in-app purchase the App offers is handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit.
  • Kynoss Studios does not receive, store, or have access to payment information at any point.
  • Purchase status is verified locally using Apple's on-device Transaction API.

Required Reason APIs

Widgii uses only Apple's first-party frameworks and does not access any APIs that require declared reasons beyond those already disclosed in this policy and in the app's App Store privacy nutrition label.

App Tracking Transparency

Widgii does not track users across apps or websites owned by other companies. The App Tracking Transparency framework is not used because no tracking occurs.

App Privacy Nutrition Label

Widgii's App Store privacy nutrition label reflects that the app does not collect data and does not track users.

11. TestFlight Beta Participation

Widgii is currently distributed as a pre-release build through Apple's TestFlight. Two things are worth stating plainly for testers:

  • The beta build stores nothing at all. It holds a board in memory for the length of a session and writes nothing to disk. Progress saving described in section 2 arrives with the released app.
  • TestFlight itself is Apple's service, not ours. When you install a beta through TestFlight, Apple provides us with aggregate installation and crash information, and any feedback you choose to submit through TestFlight — which may include a screenshot and your email address if you send it. That flow is governed by Apple's privacy policy, and it is the only channel through which any information about you reaches us. The App itself still transmits nothing.
