About Sievr

Free up gigabytes in minutes. Sievr scans your photo library, groups your near-duplicate photos, and picks the best shot in every group — so you can clean up the rest with one tap.

Burst shots, retakes, slightly different angles of the same scene — Sievr's AI groups them, scores each one for sharpness, faces, lighting, and lens smudges, and recommends which one to keep. You glance at the group, accept the pick (or override it), and move on. A 10,000-photo library cleans up in a single sitting.

Free to scan

New in 1.3.0

A safer, steadier Sievr. We went through the whole app looking for anything that could cost you a photo you meant to keep, and fixed it.

  • Sievr never trashes the photo it recommended as your best shot unless you tell it to
  • Your own Keep and Trash choices stick, and Trust Sievr no longer overrides them
  • Long full scans pick up where they left off if you leave Sievr or it closes
  • Scan New Photos now catches everything added since your last scan, and skips videos and albums you've excluded
  • Cancelling a scan asks first
  • Fairer best-shot picks: resolution is compared correctly across portrait and landscape, and faces count the way they should
  • Take a closer look at Low Quality photos before they go
  • Trash Rest asks before it trashes your best shot, and Reset whole group undoes a group in one tap
  • Storage-saved figures are accurate now
  • Widgets show your real numbers
  • Ask to Buy and Restore Purchases now tell you what happened
  • Photo Reminders are gone (they never actually sent)
  • Better VoiceOver labels, and Reduce Motion respected everywhere

Your data

  • Your photos are analyzed on your iPhone and never uploaded
